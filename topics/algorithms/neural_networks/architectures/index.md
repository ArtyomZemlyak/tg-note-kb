## Overview

This section covers various architectures used in neural networks, from traditional recurrent and convolutional architectures to modern transformer-based models and specialized architectures for specific tasks. Recent developments include advanced residual connection techniques such as Hyper-Connections and their stabilized variant Manifold-Constrained Hyper-Connections (mHC).
