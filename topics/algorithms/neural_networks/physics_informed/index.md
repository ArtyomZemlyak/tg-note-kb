## Overview

Physics-Informed Neural Networks (PINNs) are neural networks that incorporate physical laws, typically expressed as partial differential equations (PDEs), into the learning process. This approach enables the solution of forward and inverse problems involving nonlinear PDEs without the need for traditional numerical methods.
