## Advanced Concepts

### Latent Action Control
- **Learning Latent Action World Models In The Wild** - Training lightweight controllers to map robot actions to latent actions learned from unannotated video data
- Demonstrates that controllers trained on small robotic datasets can control world models learned from internet videos
- Enables planning for real robots (DROID, RECON) with performance comparable to ground-truth trained models
