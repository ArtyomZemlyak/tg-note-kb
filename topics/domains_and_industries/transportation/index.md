## Overview

AI applications in transportation focus on improving safety, efficiency, and sustainability of movement of people and goods. This section covers various aspects of intelligent transportation systems.

## Key Technologies

### Perception and Navigation
- Computer vision for environment understanding
- Sensor fusion for robust environment modeling
- Real-time decision making for navigation

### Optimization
- Route planning and optimization algorithms
- Traffic flow prediction and management
- Resource allocation for transportation networks

### Safety Systems
- Collision avoidance and risk assessment
- Behavior prediction for other traffic participants
- Emergency response and fault tolerance
