# Mathematical Foundations

Mathematics forms the theoretical backbone of machine learning, data science, and artificial intelligence. This section covers the essential mathematical concepts required to understand and develop ML/DS/AI systems.

## Overview

Understanding mathematical concepts is crucial for developing, optimizing, and interpreting ML/DS/AI models. These mathematical tools provide the framework for algorithm design, analysis, and optimization.
